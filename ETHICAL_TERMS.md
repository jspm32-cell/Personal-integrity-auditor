# Ethical Usage Terms

## Mandatory Restrictions
By using this software, you agree:

1. **Personal Use Only** - Never use to analyze, evaluate, or judge others
2. **Preserve Safety Features** - Do not remove or disable coercion-proofing
3. **Transparency** - Modifications affecting safety must be documented
4. **Non-Coercive** - Do not use for enforcement, compliance, or control

## Prohibited Applications
- Employee/student evaluation
- Social credit or scoring systems  
- Compliance enforcement
- Surveillance of any kind
- Automated decision-making about people

## Violation Consequences
Violating these terms terminates your license and may result in public notification.
